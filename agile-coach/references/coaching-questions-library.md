# Coaching Questions Library

Questions organised by the situation you're in. For event-level and team-level questions a Scrum Master uses week to week, see `scrum-master/references/coaching-questions.md` — this library is aimed at coaching individuals, leaders and organisations.

## What makes a question powerful

- **Open** — cannot be answered yes or no
- **Short** — under twelve words usually beats a paragraph
- **Free of your answer** — no recommendation smuggled in as a question
- **Focused on them** — their thinking, their options, their next step

The single most useful thing you can do after asking is nothing. Let the silence run.

**Question types, weakest to strongest:** closed → leading → clarifying → exploratory → challenging → reframing. Most coaches live in clarifying and never get to challenging.

## GROW

**Goal** — what they want from the conversation, not the project
- What would make this conversation worth your time?
- What do you want to be different by the end of this?
- If we solved one thing today, what should it be?
- What does good look like here?

**Reality** — spend longer here than feels comfortable
- What's happening now?
- What have you tried already? What happened?
- What's the impact of this on you? On the team?
- What's your part in this situation?
- What have you not tried, and why not?
- What do you already know that you're not acting on?

**Options** — quantity before quality
- What could you do?
- What else? (ask three times)
- What would you do if you had no constraints?
- What would you advise a colleague in your position?
- What's the option you've dismissed? Why?
- Who could help with this?

**Will / Way forward**
- What will you do?
- By when?
- On a scale of one to ten, how likely are you to do it? What would make it higher?
- What might get in the way?
- What support do you need, and who from?
- How will you know it worked?

## By situation

**Stuck**
- What's the one thing that, if it moved, would unblock everything else?
- If you already knew the answer, what would it be?
- What are you pretending not to know?
- What's the smallest step you could take before Friday?

**Overwhelmed**
- What's genuinely yours to carry here?
- What happens if that doesn't get done?
- What are you saying yes to that you should be saying no to?
- Where is your energy going that isn't producing anything?

**Avoiding something**
- What's the conversation you're not having?
- What are you afraid will happen?
- What happens if nothing changes?
- What would courage look like here?

**Repeating a pattern**
- How often has this happened before?
- What's your part in the pattern?
- What do you get from keeping things this way?
- What would have to change in you for this to change?

**Stuck in a story**
- What's the story you're telling yourself about why this is hard?
- What else could be true?
- What would a neutral observer see?
- How would you describe this if you liked the person involved?

**Facing a decision**
- What are you actually choosing between?
- What would you decide if you couldn't fail?
- Which option will you regret not taking?
- What does your gut say, and what's stopping you trusting it?
- What would need to be true for option B to be right?

**Low confidence**
- When have you handled something like this before?
- What would the version of you in two years' time say about this?
- Whose opinion are you carrying that isn't yours?

## Coaching leaders

- What outcome are you actually accountable for here?
- What are you doing that someone in your team could do?
- What would your team say it's like to work for you? How do you know?
- Where are you the bottleneck?
- What decisions are waiting on you right now?
- What are you rewarding, without meaning to?
- What behaviour do you need to model that you currently don't?
- What would you have to stop doing to lead this well?
- Who tells you the truth? When did they last do it?

**On delegation and control**
- What's the worst that happens if they do it their way?
- What would it take for you to trust this to someone else?
- What are you protecting by keeping this?

## Coaching at organisational level

**Diagnosing the system**
- What does this organisation reward, regardless of what it says it values?
- Where does work get stuck most often? Why there?
- What decisions have to travel furthest to get made?
- What do people say in the corridor that they don't say in the room?
- What's the gap between the story leadership tells and what teams experience?
- What would a new joiner find strange after a month?

**On change**
- What's the cost of not changing? Who bears it?
- What's the strongest argument against this change?
- Who loses if this succeeds?
- What have you tried before that didn't stick? What killed it?
- What's the smallest change that would prove this works?
- Who needs to be seen to support this, and what would make that easy for them?

**On culture and safety**
- What happens here when someone makes a mistake?
- When did someone last challenge the most senior person in a room, and what happened to them?
- What can't be said here?
- What would have to be true for people to raise problems earlier?

## Challenging questions — use sparingly, once trust exists

- You've told me this twice now. What's really going on?
- What are you getting out of not solving this?
- You've said "they won't let me" three times. What's yours in this?
- What would you have to admit for that to be true?
- Is this a problem you want solved, or a problem you want heard?
- What's the conversation we're avoiding right now?

Challenge lands when the relationship can carry it and when it's delivered with genuine warmth. Delivered without either, it's just criticism with a question mark.

---

## Closing a coaching conversation

- What's different for you now compared with when we started?
- What are you taking away?
- What will you do first?
- What do you want me to ask you about next time?

## Questions for your own supervision

- Whose agenda was that conversation serving?
- Where did I give advice when a question would have served better?
- What did I avoid asking, and why?
- What am I attached to in this client's outcome?
- Am I still useful here, or have I become part of the furniture?
