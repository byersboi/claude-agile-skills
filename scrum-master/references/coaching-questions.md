# Coaching Questions for Scrum Masters

A working library for team-level coaching. For the coaching *stance* — when to ask versus tell, contracting, GROW structure — see the `agile-coach` skill; for questions aimed at the profession and organisation, see `agile-coach/references/coaching-questions-library.md`. This file is the one to open five minutes before a session.

## How to use a question well

- **Ask one. Then stop.** Stacking two questions lets the person answer the easier one
- **Silence is the technique**, not a gap in it. Count to seven before you fill it
- **Open, short, and about them** — "what" and "how" open thinking; "why" often triggers justification
- **Follow the energy, not your plan.** The question you prepared is rarely the one the answer calls for

Avoid: leading questions with an answer baked in ("don't you think we should...?"), and rhetorical challenges dressed as curiosity.

## Sprint Planning

- What would make this Sprint a success, beyond the items being done?
- If we could only finish one thing this Sprint, what would it be?
- What has to be true for this plan to hold?
- What are we assuming about capacity this Sprint?
- What's the riskiest item here, and what would reduce the risk fastest?
- Who's going to be a single point of failure if we plan it this way?
- Is this a plan we believe in, or a plan we've accepted?

## Daily Scrum

- What's the one thing that would most help us hit the Sprint Goal today?
- What's been in progress longest? What's it waiting on?
- Who needs help and hasn't asked for it?
- What did we learn yesterday that changes today's plan?
- If we carry on exactly like this, do we make the Sprint Goal?

## Backlog Refinement

- What problem is this item solving, and for whom?
- How would we know this was a success once it's live?
- What's the smallest version of this that would deliver value?
- What would we have to learn to size this confidently?
- What are we not building by building this?

## Sprint Review

- What surprised you about how this turned out?
- What did we learn about the users this Sprint?
- What would you change about this if you built it again?
- (To stakeholders) What would you have wanted to see instead?
- (To stakeholders) What does this change about what matters next?

## Retrospective

**Opening the field:**
- What word describes this Sprint for you?
- What's the thing nobody has said yet?
- If you could rerun one day of this Sprint, which and why?

**Going deeper when the discussion stays safe:**
- We've raised this before — what's kept it from changing?
- What's the cost of leaving this as it is?
- What would we have to give up to fix this?
- What's within our control here, and what isn't?
- What are we tolerating that we shouldn't be?

**Converting to action:**
- What's one thing we can change before the next retro?
- Who's going to do that, and when?
- What would tell us in two weeks that it worked?
- What support do you need to make that happen?

## Coaching individuals

**Someone who is stuck:**
- What have you already tried?
- What would you advise a colleague in this position?
- What's the smallest next step you could take today?
- If you already knew the answer, what would it be?

**Someone who is overloaded:**
- What's on your plate right now? Which of those is genuinely yours?
- What would happen if that didn't get done this week?
- What do you need to say no to, and who needs to hear it?

**Someone avoiding a difficult conversation:**
- What's the conversation you're not having?
- What are you afraid will happen if you have it?
- What happens if you don't?
- What's the first sentence you'd use?

**Someone disengaged:**
- What's the best bit of your work right now?
- When did you last feel proud of something you shipped?
- What's changed for you over the last few months?
- What would have to be different for this to be worth your energy?

## Coaching the team as a system

- What does this team do better than any other you've worked in?
- Where does work get stuck most often? Why there?
- What decisions does this team make for itself? Which does it wait for?
- What would this team do differently if nobody outside it was watching?
- What do we do here because it makes sense, and what do we do because we always have?
- If a new person joined tomorrow, what would confuse them most?

## Coaching the Product Owner

- How does this item connect to the Product Goal?
- What evidence do we have that this is the most valuable thing next?
- What would you drop to make room for this?
- What are you hearing from stakeholders that the team hasn't heard?
- Who says no to this if it's not right?

## Coaching upward — managers and stakeholders

- What outcome are you trying to achieve? What would tell you you'd got it?
- What decision are you trying to make with this information?
- What's the impact on you if this slips a fortnight?
- What would you want the team to stop doing to make this happen?
- What's the constraint you're under that I might not be seeing?

## When psychological safety is low

Start indirect. People answer about "the team" long before they answer about themselves.

- What would someone new notice about how this team works?
- What do teams like ours usually struggle with?
- On a scale of one to ten, how safe does it feel to disagree here? What would make it one higher?
- What's something you've thought about raising but decided not to?
- If nothing changes in six months, what's the cost?

Write-first formats beat verbal rounds here every time — see the `facilitation` skill.

## Questions to ask yourself

- Whose problem am I solving right now — theirs or mine?
- Am I asking because I'm curious, or because I want them to reach my answer?
- What would happen if I did nothing here for a week?
- What is this team no longer able to do without me?
- What am I avoiding?
